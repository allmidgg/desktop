The receipts

125,096 games is a claim. This is what it is made of.

There is no panel of experts here and no model. There is one file — matches.jsonl, a line per finished game, written by the app as people play — and everything on this site is counted out of it. So here are its books, including the counters that make the sample look smaller than it is.

Game time behind the numbers 62,534 hours games × mean length — 7.1 years of League

Every figure above is a counter kept while the file was read, or arithmetic on two of them. The same totals drive the tier lists, the builds and the app itself. See what they add up to.
