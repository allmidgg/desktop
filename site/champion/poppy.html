No win rate here yet, and that is the honest answer

Every number on AllMid comes from games we recorded ourselves. Our data covers League Classic, the Season 3 remake — and Poppy was not in the game in Season 3, so there is no Classic match that could carry a statistic about this champion. That is a fact about the mode, not a gap we are hiding.

Standard League queues are next. When there is a real sample behind Poppy, the builds, counters and lane numbers land on this page — with the sample size attached, the same as everywhere else here. Until then this page carries only what Riot publishes.
